Efficient Quota Management Solutions

Ensure seamless compliance with UAE labor laws by managing workforce quotas effortlessly. Tah-zeel provides expert support to help you maintain approvals, adjust quotas, and meet regulatory requirements with ease.

Quota management is essential for maintaining the required workforce under UAE labor laws. It involves obtaining permissions from the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) to recruit a specific number of employees based on a business’s category, size, and industry regulations. Managing quotas helps ensure compliance with labor laws and minimizes the risk of legal complications, especially as employee requirements may evolve with business growth or restructuring.

At Tah-zeel, we streamline this process by coordinating directly with MOHRE, managing applications for new quota approvals, and handling renewals or adjustments to align with your business needs. Our team offers personalized guidance, ensuring that each application meets the latest government standards, helping businesses maintain legal compliance and flexibility as they grow.
