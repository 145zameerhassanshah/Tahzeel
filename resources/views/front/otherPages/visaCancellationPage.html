Understanding the Need for Cancellation

Key Situations Requiring Visa Cancellation

Visa cancellation is mandatory for those planning to leave the UAE permanently, switching employment, or transferring to a new visa. Tah-zeel assists in determining if and when a cancellation is necessary, minimizing potential issues.

End of Employment

Required when employment terminates.

Permanent Exit

Necessary for residents planning to leave the UAE for good.

Family Visa Holders:

Sponsors must cancel dependents' visas before their own.

Switching Residency Status

For individuals changing visa categories or sponsors.
